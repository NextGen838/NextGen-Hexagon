Privacy Policy — NEXTGEN HEXAGON

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy applies to the NEXTGEN HEXAGON add-in for Autodesk Fusion ("the App"), published by [PUBLISHER NAME] ("we," "us," "Publisher"). It describes what data, if any, the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data Collection and Use

NEXTGEN HEXAGON does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or design data.

  • The App runs entirely locally within your Fusion session.
  • The App reads geometry (sketch profiles and curves) that you explicitly select within your own Fusion document, and writes new sketch geometry into that same document. This data never leaves your local Fusion session.
  • The App makes no network requests and does not connect to any external server.
  • The App does not use analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • The App does not access, collect, or store your name, email address, account information, device identifiers, location, or any other personal information.
  • The App does not access files or data outside of the active Fusion document you are working in.

The only feedback the App produces is local, informational log messages within Fusion's own logging console (e.g., confirming how many hexagons were generated). These messages remain on your local machine and are not accessible to the Publisher.

2. Third-Party Data Sharing

Because the App does not collect any data, it does not share any data with any third party. No third-party analytics providers, advertising networks, SDKs, or affiliates receive any data from this App, since none is collected in the first place.

Should this ever change in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance of the change, the update will be reflected in both the App Store listing and the copy bundled with the App, and any named third party would be contractually required to provide at least the same level of data protection described in this Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

The Publisher does not collect or receive any data from the App, and therefore holds no user data related to the App's use. There is nothing for the Publisher to retain or delete, as no data is transmitted to or stored by the Publisher at any point.

Any geometry created by the App exists only within your own local Fusion document and is fully under your control — you may edit or delete it at any time using standard Fusion functionality (undo, delete, or removing the sketch geometry).

4. Revoking Consent / Requesting Data Deletion

Because the App does not collect personal data or design data, there is no data held by the Publisher to revoke consent for or request deletion of.
